G-Code Forge is an offline Android workspace for CNC G-code.

Use Pattern Repeater, Hexagonal Flat Converter and Two Axis Converter in one saved file. Set parameters, generate a program, inspect its path, and copy or export an NC file.

Programs, settings, presets and recovery history are stored locally in SQLite. No account, backend, advertising or analytics integration is required. Import NC, G-code and text files, or back up and restore your workspaces as JSON.

The editor includes syntax colors, undo/redo, find/replace and jump to line. Compare generated output with the source, explore the toolpath with touch gestures and playback, and switch between light and dark themes.

The preview is a visual aid and does not provide collision checking, stock removal or controller-accurate simulation. Always review and simulate generated code against your machine setup before machining.
